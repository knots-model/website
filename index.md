<div class="about" markdown="1">
## The next generation of shipping models

KNOTS brings together three traditions that have long worked apart:

- **Bottom-up emission inventories**, which build fleet totals from the activity of each individual ship;
- **Ship engineering practice**, which describes how hulls, propellers and engines turn energy into speed, in real wind and waves;
- **Systems analysis**, which follows how fleets, trade, fuels and technologies change together over time.

Building on the MariTEAM model, KNOTS combines global ship tracks (AIS), weather, port calls and ship particulars to calculate the energy, fuel and emissions of every voyage of the world fleet. That gives a detailed picture of shipping today, and a foundation for asking how it can change.
